How it works

  1. Add what you pay forEnter the name, price, how often it bills, and the next renewal date. It takes about ten seconds each.
  2. See the real totalMonthly and yearly cost, a category breakdown, and your most expensive subscription.
  3. Act before it renewsRenewals are sorted by date, every subscription has a cancel-page link, and you can add a calendar reminder.

Why no bank link?

Your data stays with you

Subscriptions are stored in your browser. There is no account to hack and no bank connection to revoke.

No tricks to cancel

Subtract links you to the service’s own cancel page. It doesn’t charge you to cancel and doesn’t negotiate on your behalf.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, the tracker is free to use. A paid plan with extras like email reminders and sync is planned, and the free plan will keep covering up to 5 subscriptions.

Where is my data stored?

In your own browser, on your device. Subtract doesn’t receive it. That also means clearing your browser data erases it, so use the backup option in the app.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open the page in your phone’s browser. Add it to your home screen for quick access.

Will it cancel subscriptions for me?

No. It shows you what you pay, reminds you before renewals, and links to each service’s cancel page. You stay in control.
